Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Planora is designed to help users manage tasks, notes, reminders and schedules securely.

1. Information We Handle

Planora is designed primarily as a productivity application. Tasks, notes, calendar information and related productivity content are primarily stored locally on your device according to the current application architecture.

When you create or use an authenticated account, Planora may process account information required to provide authentication and account-related functionality.

2. Authentication

Planora currently supports account authentication using Email/Password, Google Sign-In and Anonymous/Guest authentication through Firebase Authentication.

  • Email/Password accounts may include the user's email address and profile information provided during account setup.
  • Google Sign-In may provide basic profile information such as name, email address and profile photo where available.
  • Anonymous/Guest accounts use an anonymous Firebase identity and may use a locally generated Planora guest name.

3. Local App Data

Planora uses local storage for application functionality. This may include tasks, notes, calendar information, settings and other locally stored productivity data.

Users are responsible for maintaining backups of information they consider important.

4. Notifications

Planora may request notification permission to provide reminders and productivity notifications. Notification permission can be managed through Android device settings and the application's available notification controls.

5. Analytics and Platform Services

Limited analytics and platform services may be used to understand application performance, reliability and usage. The specific services enabled may change as the application develops.

Planora does not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information handled by Planora. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Account Deletion

Users with a Planora account can initiate account deletion from within the application through the account settings. Planora also provides an external account deletion resource through this Legal Center.

When an account deletion request is completed, the associated account information is deleted as described in the application's deletion flow and applicable service requirements.

Certain information may be retained where retention is required or permitted for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention or regulatory purposes.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Planora's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Account Control

Delete Your Planora Account

This page explains how Planora users can request deletion of their account and associated account information.

⚠
Account deletion is permanent. Once an account is successfully deleted, the account cannot be restored through the normal sign-in process.

Delete from the Planora App

If you can access your Planora account, the primary deletion path is available directly inside the application:

1
Open Planora

Launch the Planora application and sign in to your account.

2
Open Settings

Go to the Settings section and open your account/profile area.

3
Choose Delete Account

Select the Delete Account option and review the confirmation message.

4
Confirm Deletion

Confirm the deletion request. Authentication may be required again for security-sensitive account deletion operations.

5
Account Deletion

After successful verification, the Planora Firebase authentication account is deleted and the user is returned to the authentication screen.

Supported Account Types

  • Email/Password accounts
  • Google accounts
  • Anonymous/Guest accounts

What Happens After Deletion?

The Planora account associated with the deleted Firebase authentication identity is removed through the account deletion process.

Account-specific profile/authentication information is removed according to the application's deletion flow.

If additional cloud services or synchronized data are added to Planora in the future, the applicable deletion process and this policy will be updated accordingly.

Need Help With Account Deletion?

If you cannot complete the account deletion process from inside the app, contact Planora Support using the email address below and include enough information for us to understand your request. Do not send your password or authentication credentials.
